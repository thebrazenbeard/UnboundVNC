home computers for unrelated personal purposes.

[GitHub](https://github.com/thebrazenbeard) and describe:

1. The exact UnboundVNC version, commit, or release.
2. The intended business activity, number of users/devices, and distribution

3. The desired commercial term and support needs.

public price or automatic commercial entitlement is implied. Silence, public
GitHub access, forks, or prior personal-use permission are not commercial
licenses.
